# Data status and provenance

The atlas bundles **no real health records, precise sensitive locations, satellite pixels, model weights, source-course datasets or third-party screenshots**. Default notebooks read no remote APIs or data services. Original numeric fixtures and authored JSON/STAC-like records are synthetic and explicitly labeled. They do not estimate disease burden, causal effects, real service performance or live AI capability.

## Ocean Watch and Earth studio refresh

The public inventory now contains 59 records; the curated atlas has 30 JupyterLite-named and three related projects. The source refresh adds 51 evidenced entries; the full catalog has **134** dated launch resources (**132 HTTP 200**, two retained HTTP 404), including all thirty exact Earth-course notebook launchers. Reachability does not validate query targets or external execution. Prior audit rows remain intact.

The Ocean Watch candidate fixture invents four SAR detections and five AIS positions in planar km/minutes; two inclusive gates preserve unique-candidate, ambiguous and unmatched categories over all four detections. It estimates no real identity, fleet coverage or legality. The cube fixture invents two cells, 8/5-day intervals, hectares and Celsius, preserving zero and per-variable None masks; covered cell-days support an observed extensive total and a duration-weighted weather mean. All-missing metrics remain unknown. It is a Python analogue of SQL; neither SQL nor LexCube is executed here.

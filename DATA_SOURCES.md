# Data status and provenance

The atlas bundles **no real health records, precise sensitive locations, satellite pixels, model weights, source-course datasets or third-party screenshots**. Default notebooks read no remote APIs or data services. Original numeric fixtures and authored JSON/STAC-like records are synthetic and explicitly labeled. They do not estimate disease burden, causal effects, real service performance or live AI capability.

The Global Health chapter transcribes all ten exact lesson launch URLs from the Rewiring source README. That source distinguishes synthetic grids, actual STAC metadata, uncalibrated teaching models and optional services. This atlas links those works; it does not redistribute them or reproduce source claims as observed results. References to IHME/GBD, Stanford HIVDB, GHSL, AR6, GeoTessera, ACLED, OSM and source models are context/continuation links, not bundled products.

The refreshed discovery contains only public records. All four requested source visibility changes are complete and their chapters are included. Original/revised/old **public** variants remain distinct. Shiny-Live-Apps has only a title-only README; the corresponding threshold-rule prototype is original atlas code, not a supplied Shiny app. The new spatial exercises use invented line cells, planar points and selection fixtures; no source teaching extracts are redistributed. The atlas contains 32 project chapters and 41 canonical notebooks. Updates require a new reviewed public inventory/README check and explicit provenance fields. There is no unattended data refresh or source scraping in the normal build.

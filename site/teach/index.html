What can this selection support?

Terrain heights, population estimates, satellite samples, published reporting counts and gauge observations are independent quantities. Their epochs and datums cannot be combined by adding them. There are no facility locations in these views. A height threshold is not inundation, hydraulic connectivity, people at risk or a 2030 forecast.
